Members Management

Late joining par member ko pichlay tamam months ke Rs. 3,000 per month arrears dene honge. Joining month ki Rs. 3,000 contribution bhi due hogi. Monthly committee target hamesha active members × per-member contribution hoga.

Inactive karne se member ka purana record محفوظ rehta hai. Delete permanently karne se member aur uski tamam payment/recovery entries remove ho jayengi; is se purani history aur totals bhi recalculate honge. Pehle backup lein.

Monthly Payments
