Committee Settings

Settings ko save karne se monthly target automatically update hoga. Months ya contribution change karne se purani entries delete nahi hotin.

Members Management

Late joining par pichlay tamam months ki contribution arrears ke taur par joining month mein due hogi. Member tabhi add hoga jab ye arrears receive ho kar record kar di jayen. Joining month ki normal contribution bhi alag due hogi. Active members × monthly contribution se regular committee target calculate hota hai; arrears is target se alag extra collection hain.

Inactive karne se member ka purana record محفوظ rehta hai. Delete permanently karne se member aur uski tamam payment/recovery entries remove ho jayengi; is se purani history aur totals bhi recalculate honge. Pehle backup lein.

Monthly Payments

Har member ke liye is month ki payment aur purane baqaya ki recovery enter karein. Payment type karte hi valid amount auto-save hota hai. Monthly target = active members × per-member contribution.

Excel / Google Sheets Export

CSV file Excel mein open hoti hai aur Google Sheets mein import ki ja sakti hai. CSV mein sirf Member Name, Month, Pending Payment, Total IN aur Total OUT honge. Har member ka month-wise record alag row mein hoga.

Important: Export file download karne ke baad usay open karke check karein. Purana record sirf aapke alag confirmation ke baad website se clear hoga. Browser download ko website khud verify nahi kar sakti.
